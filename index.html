Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Kunai Strike ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Kunai Strike does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require an internet connection to play and does not contain any account creation, sign-in, or data submission features.

Automatically Collected Information

The App does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services of any kind. No usage data, device identifiers, or diagnostic information is collected by the App itself.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, or software development kits (SDKs) that collect user data. If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly before such changes take effect.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected by the App, no personal information from children is gathered, used, or disclosed.

Data Storage

Any game progress, settings, or preferences are stored locally on your device only. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party, and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its local storage.

Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal information at risk of exposure through our systems.

Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) Compliance

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The App does not collect personal information from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Consent

By using the App, you consent to this Privacy Policy.
